# 1. Introduction

The AI-Commerce Platform Technology Radar defines the strategic technology landscape adopted by the platform.

Its purpose is to guide technology decisions, reduce unnecessary complexity, and establish a controlled approach for adopting new technologies.

Technology choices must be driven by:

- business requirements;
- architectural principles;
- operational maturity;
- security considerations;
- long-term sustainability.

The Technology Radar prevents technology adoption based only on trends.
