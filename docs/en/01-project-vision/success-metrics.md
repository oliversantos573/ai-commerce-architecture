# Success Metrics

## Overview

Success Metrics define how the AI-Commerce Platform measures progress toward its business, architectural, engineering, and operational objectives.

These metrics establish objective indicators that validate whether the platform is delivering the expected value while maintaining high engineering standards.

The metrics presented in this document serve as the baseline for continuous improvement and architectural governance.

---

# Objectives

The platform measures success across four dimensions:

- Business Outcomes
- Engineering Excellence
- Platform Operations
- Architecture Quality

Each dimension contains measurable Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) monitored throughout the software lifecycle.

---
