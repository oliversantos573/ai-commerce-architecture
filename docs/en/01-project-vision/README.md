# Overview

The **Project Vision** module establishes the strategic foundation of the AI-Commerce Platform.

Rather than describing implementation details, this module explains **why the platform exists**, **which business problems it solves**, **who the stakeholders are**, and **which long-term objectives guide every architectural decision**.

All subsequent Architecture Design Documents (ADDs) are derived from the principles defined in this module.

This module serves as the starting point for the entire architecture documentation.

---

# Objectives

After completing this module, readers will understand:

- The strategic purpose of the platform.
- The business problems addressed.
- The long-term vision and mission.
- Business objectives and expected outcomes.
- Stakeholder responsibilities.
- Project scope and boundaries.
- Success metrics.
- Architectural governance principles.
