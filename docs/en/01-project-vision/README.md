## 1. Introduction
📚 **Concept**: Introduce the purpose of the platform and its strategic importance.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Companies like Amazon and Microsoft start by clarifying the "why" before the "how".  
🎯 **Application**: Explain why AI-Commerce exists and the business problem it solves.

## 2. Vision
📚 **Concept**: A clear statement of the future state the platform aims to achieve.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Vision documents describe the long-term impact.  
🎯 **Application**: Define how AI agents and microservices will transform e-commerce.

## 3. Mission
📚 **Concept**: The actionable path to achieve the vision.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Mission statements guide daily execution.  
🎯 **Application**: Outline how the platform will deliver intelligent commerce experiences.

## 4. Business Problem
📚 **Concept**: Identify the pain points in current e-commerce solutions.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Architecture documents always start from the business challenge.  
🎯 **Application**: Describe inefficiencies, lack of personalization, and scalability issues.

## 5. Target Audience
📚 **Concept**: Define who benefits from the platform.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Stakeholders include customers, developers, and business teams.  
🎯 **Application**: Identify merchants, consumers, and AI engineers.

## 6. Platform Scope
📚 **Concept**: Boundaries of the system.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Scope prevents uncontrolled expansion.  
🎯 **Application**: Clarify what the platform will and will not do.

## 7. Functional Goals
📚 **Concept**: Capabilities the system must deliver.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Goals are measurable and testable.  
🎯 **Application**: Intelligent product recommendations, dynamic pricing, automated customer support.

## 8. Non-Functional Goals
📚 **Concept**: Quality attributes of the system.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Availability, scalability, performance, security, observability, maintainability.  
🎯 **Application**: Define SLAs, compliance, and resilience targets.

## 9. High-Level Architecture
📚 **Concept**: Overview of the system design.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Architecture diagrams show components and interactions.  
🎯 **Application**: Describe AI agents, microservices, event-driven communication, and cloud infrastructure.

## 10. Technology Stack
📚 **Concept**: Tools and frameworks chosen.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Stack decisions are justified by scalability and support.  
🎯 **Application**: Languages, frameworks, databases, cloud providers.

## 11. Expected Outcomes
📚 **Concept**: What success looks like.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Outcomes are tied to ROI and KPIs.  
🎯 **Application**: Increased conversion rates, reduced operational costs, improved customer satisfaction.

## 12. Roadmap
📚 **Concept**: Timeline of evolution.  
🏢 **Industry Practice**: Roadmaps align technical milestones with business goals.  
🎯 **Application**: Phases of delivery, from MVP to global rollout.
