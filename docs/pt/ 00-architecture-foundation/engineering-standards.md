# 1. Introduction

The AI-Commerce Platform Engineering Standards define the technical guidelines, development practices, and quality expectations required to build and maintain an enterprise-grade intelligent commerce ecosystem.

These standards establish a common engineering language across development teams and ensure consistency in:

- software design;
- implementation practices;
- testing strategy;
- deployment processes;
- operational excellence.

The objective is to create software that is:

- reliable;
- secure;
- scalable;
- maintainable;
- observable.

---

# 2. Engineering Philosophy

The platform follows these engineering values:

## Quality First

Software quality must be considered from the beginning of development.

## Simplicity

Solutions should avoid unnecessary complexity.

## Automation

Manual processes should be replaced whenever possible.

## Continuous Improvement

Engineering practices continuously evolve through feedback and measurement.

## Ownership

Teams are responsible for the complete lifecycle of their services.

---

# 3. Software Design Standards

## 3.1 Domain-Driven Design (DDD)

Business logic must be modeled around business capabilities.

The platform follows:

- bounded contexts;
- aggregates;
- entities;
- value objects;
- domain events.
